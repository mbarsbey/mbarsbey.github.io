I am a postdoctoral researcher at **<span style="color: #d600be">Imperial College London</span>**, working on **<span style="color: #d68300">reliable and efficient ML</span>** under real-world constraints. My current research examines the **<span style="color: #d68300">stability, safety, and alignment of LLMs and VLMs</span>** under resource-efficiency pressures, and how representation geometry mediates this relationship. At Imperial, I have also worked on **<span style="color: #d68300">mechanistic interpretability</span>**, **<span style="color: #d68300">learning dynamics</span>**, and robustness to **<span style="color: #d68300">spurious correlations</span>**. My research has been featured in **<span style="color: #d600be">Nature Medicine</span>**, **<span style="color: #d600be">NeurIPS</span>**, **<span style="color: #d600be">ICML</span>**, **<span style="color: #d600be">ICLR</span>**, and **<span style="color: #d600be">ICCV</span>**, among others.

Before Imperial, I completed my PhD at **<span style="color: #d600be">Boğaziçi University</span>**, where my research focused on **<span style="color: #d68300">robust generalization</span>** in deep learning and **<span style="color: #d68300">probabilistic latent variable models</span>**. During my PhD, I was a research scientist intern at **<span style="color: #d600be">Google DeepMind</span>**, where I worked on [**<span style="color: #d68300">human-AI complementarity</span>**](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02437-x) in medical diagnosis and on evaluating medical AI systems under [**<span style="color: #d68300">uncertain ground truth</span>**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1361841525001033).

I have **<span style="color: #d600be">industry experience</span>** in the research, deployment, and team leadership of **<span style="color: #d68300">production ML systems</span>**, most notably leading a team behind daily cash-demand forecasting for 8,000+ ATMs.
